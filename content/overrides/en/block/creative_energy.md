# Creative energy source
`oc2r:creative_energy`

Supplies energy to adjacent consumers. It is available in creative mode for testing equipment and has no survival recipe. Place a computer, charger or another energy consumer next to it.

[Wiki](https://wiki.loki-code.dev/en/recipes).
